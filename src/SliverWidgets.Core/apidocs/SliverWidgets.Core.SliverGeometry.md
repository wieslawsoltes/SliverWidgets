# Summary
Reports the aggregate scroll, paint, cache, hit-test, obstruction, overflow, and visibility behavior of a sliver.

# Remarks
`SliverGeometry` is validated against the constraints that produced it. Invalid negative extents, paint extents larger than the remaining paint range, or layout extents larger than paint extents are rejected early.
